# Superstore SQL Analysis

**Project Overview**

This project analyzes sales and profitability data from a fictional retail superstore using SQL.
The analysis focuses on understanding the company's sales performance, identifying profitable and unprofitable products, evaluating customer segments, and examining regional performance.
Using transaction-level data, the project explores how product categories, discounts, shipping methods, customer segments, and geographic locations relate to sales and profit.
The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can help identify areas for improvement and support better business decisions.

**Business Context**

With increasing competition in the retail industry, businesses need to understand which products, customer segments, and regions contribute most to their overall performance.
A superstore wants to understand what drives its sales and profitability. It needs to identify which products and regions perform well, which areas generate losses, and where improvements can be made.
This project uses SQL to analyze transaction data and Power BI to present the results through an interactive dashboard.

**Project Objectives**

This project aims to:

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Identify the best-performing and least-performing product categories.
- Analyze sales and profit across different regions and states.
- Examine customer segments and their contribution to sales.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Identify products and sub-categories generating losses.
- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Evaluate shipping performance and shipping methods.
- Develop data-driven recommendations to improve business performance.

**Key Business Questions**

The analysis will aim to answer the following questions:

- How much revenue and profit does the superstore generate?
- Which product categories contribute the most to total sales?
- Which categories and sub-categories generate the highest profit?
- Which products generate the highest and lowest profit?
- Which regions have the strongest sales performance?
- Are there regions with high sales but low profitability?
- Which customer segment contributes the most to revenue?
- How do discounts affect product profitability?
- Which shipping methods are most commonly used?
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- What areas should the business prioritize to improve profitability?
